## Design Context

### Users

Product, UX, QA, and engineering stakeholders evaluating mobile user journeys. They need to understand Flowy's value quickly in a demo, product review, or pitch: what it captures, what it reveals, and how the insights become actionable.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, intelligent. The experience should evoke confidence, clarity, and a sense of effortless technical sophistication.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined Apple-style editorial minimalism: generous white space, precise typography, thin neutral borders, restrained Flowy blue accents, and real product screenshots. Avoid generic SaaS gradients, noisy dashboards, dark neon AI aesthetics, and repetitive icon-card grids.

### Design Principles

1. Show the product evidence, not abstract promises.
2. Make complex capture and analysis feel effortless.
3. Use hierarchy and whitespace before decoration.
4. Keep technical depth legible at a glance.
5. Favor a memorable editorial composition over a uniform grid.
